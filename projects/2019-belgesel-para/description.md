This is a documentary about the history of the economy and money itself.

From the publisher:
"The historical and cultural adventure of money from past to future..."

It is a documentary in which we examine the adventure of money and an element of value, from a common acceptance object that people need due to a value gap in exchanging objects in their hands to today's digital money. I think it is a valuable work that examines not only an abstract concept, but also the concrete structure of money and its features from its forms.

Para was aired on TRT2 (TV Channel) in 2021.
