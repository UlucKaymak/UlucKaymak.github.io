Anla Ki was an alternative-rock band from Istanbul which is full of lovely people. Unfortunately it didn't went well.

These are the works from 2024 to 2025 for my friend's band Anla Ki.        
I made album covers for:
* Artık Ben Yokum
* Gün Gelir
* Yaşadıkça
* Olur Olmadık
Also I edited and colored their Music Video for Geceler.
