Hi! I'm

UluçKaymak

A person who loves and creating new things.

I am a multidisciplinary artist who combines conceptual art with the pieces of the culture I was born and raised in, figurative points in audio and/or visual, interactive, and algorithmic forms. My projects often revolve around themes such as human perception, experience, and psychology.

On my personal projects, I work on fractured emotions and thoughts through various forms of digital projection, focusing on interactive audio-visual experience.

about_me.txt

As a digital artist, I create conceptual audio/visual pieces shaped by human perception, experience, and psychology.

As a composer, I design soundscapes that resonate with how people feel.

As a sound designer, I craft personal works that people categorize as simulations of mental disorder.

As a person, I love ducks.
